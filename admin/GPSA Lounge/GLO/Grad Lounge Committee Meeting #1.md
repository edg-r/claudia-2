## **General Terms of Use: Lobby Space**

* **Eligible Users:** Currently-enrolled UC San Diego graduate or professional school students whose access has been authorized by GPSA VP Campus.
  * All users must have in their possession a valid UC San Diego Graduate or Professional School ID card that clearly indicates current graduate or professional school student status while using the space. On request, users must present a valid UC San Diego ID card to GPSA staff, campus police, or other campus security officers to verify eligibility.
* **Regular Hours:** TBD
* **Summer and Holiday Hours:**  TBD
* **Exam Week Hours:** TBD
* **Room Use:** For individual, or small group use only. Silence cell phones and take/make calls in the breezeway or outside. The space is intended to be a 3rd place for graduate students, so lively conversation is encouraged. Utilization of projectors is a first come first serve basis no longer than 1 hour per person. Should you desire a more private study space or meeting area refer to the Conference Room Reservation. Do not move large furniture such as sofas, lamps, and bookshelves in the Lobby Space.
* **Food & Drink:** Food: Sturdy, sealable containers are preferred
  * Beverages: Spill-proof containers with lids are preferred
  * Clean up: Leave areas clean of any food or drink items. Trash and recycling bins are available throughout the lounge.
  * In compliance with California State Law, UC San Diego policy prohibits alcohol consumption in the space.
* **Sleeping:** UC San Diego policy (PPM 516-10.3) prohibits using the space for sleeping or living quarters, camping equipment (tents, sleeping bags), and furniture arrangements designed for sleeping.
* **Bulletin Board:**  A large bulletin board will be installed in the lobby. Posted flyers must contain the date they were posted and must be no larger than 8.27 inches x 11.69 inches in dimension. The Bulletin board will be free for all purposes, personal or organizational, unless they violate UCSD student conduct, in which they will be immediately removed. All flyers will be removed after 30 days of posting by a graduate lounge staff member, or if a flyer is found to violate the above conditions.
* **Lost & Found:** The Lost & Found will be for any non-perishable items left in the space, such as clean tupperware in the kitchen,
* **Security & Access:** Call 911 for emergencies. Call Library Safety & Security at 858-282-3602 for other concerns. If there is no answer, call the UC Campus Police at 858-534-4357. Use your own access card to enter the breezeway and Study Lounge. Don’t provide access to any other individuals and don't prop doors open or otherwise prevent the normal operation of the doors. CCTV system in operation.
* **Recommendations and Fixit Requests:** Students can use the QR codes posted within the lounge to request fixes to the facility, as well as recommendations for changes or improvements to the space.

## **General Terms of Use: Kitchen**

##

Purpose: To provide access for graduate students, many of whom commute to campus, for space to prepare food, short term storage of food, space to meet new people, and uphold a clean leave-no-trace environment.

Kitchen Facilities: The kitchen consists of a fridge & freezer, sink with drying rack, reusable ceramic plates and mugs, a microwave, and an electric tea kettle. The sink will be stocked with dish soap, sponges, and paper towels.

Users: All those who may access the student lounge may use the kitchen. Abuse of the kitchen space warrants revoking of access to the lounge.

**Sink, Dish, Fridge, and Microwave Usage:**
*Users of the kitchen are responsible for:*

* Cleaning, washing, drying, AND putting away all reusable kitchen items
* Cleaning any spilled food on the counters or in the microwave
* Marking any stored food items in the fridge or freezer with their full name and date
* Reporting broken items via the QR codes

**Contingencies:**

* **No food grease or foreign substances down the drain**
* **Sink is for the use of washing dirty dishes, washing hands, or disposal of unwanted beverages, not for disposal of solid food or foreign substances.**
* **In the event of a power outage longer than 5 hours, *an email to the lounge user-list will be sent to notify the food will have spoiled and will be promptly disposed of,* AND all perishable food left in the fridge will be disposed of immediately by a graduate student lounge representative.**
* **In the event of a power outage longer than 48 hours, *an email to the lounge user-list will be sent to notify the food will have spoiled and will be promptly disposed of, AND* all items left in the freezer will be disposed of at the end of 48 hours by a graduate student lounge representative.**

**Kitchen Maintenance:**
Every other Friday before 7pm, facilities will dispose of all fridge contents by composting old food, disposing of containings or recycling them if applicable. Any reusable items (for example a lunch-box or clean tupper ware) belonging to an individual will be left in the **student lounge lost-and-found. If payment for this extra item is not fiscally feasible, this action will be undertaken by one of two graduate student lounge representatives.**

## **Access of the Space**

The Graduate Student Lounge is available for use by UC San Diego graduate and professional students and registered graduate student organizations. Access to the lounge is a privilege, not a right. All users are expected to help keep the space safe, clean, respectful, and welcoming.

Individuals and registered graduate student organizations must review and agree to the Graduate Student Lounge Terms of Use before being granted access. Users must also complete the required Kuali form before access is approved. The Kuali form may be used to request general lounge access, event use, or both.

A separate Kuali form must be submitted for each event or reservation request, even if the individual or organization already has general access to the lounge. This helps ensure that the space is used fairly and that all events follow lounge rules.

Requests will be reviewed by \[designated lounge administrator\]. Approval may depend on the type of request, the purpose of use, space availability, and whether the individual or organization has followed lounge rules in the past.

Once access is approved, the individual student or registered student organization is responsible for following all Terms of Use while using the lounge. This includes cleaning up after use, respecting other users, protecting lounge property, and not allowing unauthorized people to enter the space.

##

## **Removal of Access**

Access to the Graduate Student Lounge may be removed if an individual student or registered student organization does not follow the Terms of Use. Access may also be removed if the space is misused or if there are concerns related to safety, security, property damage, cleanliness, or repeated rule violations.

Examples of actions that may lead to removal of access include, but are not limited to:

* Damaging lounge property
* Leaving the space dirty or unsafe
* Allowing unauthorized people to enter the lounge
* Using the lounge for an unapproved event or purpose
* Creating a safety or security concern
* Repeatedly failing to follow lounge rules
* Violating the Graduate Student Lounge Terms of Use


For less serious concerns, \[designated lounge administrator\] may issue a written warning by email before access is removed. The warning should explain the concern and what must change. If the issue continues, the individual or organization may be reviewed for removal of access.

For serious concerns, access may be removed immediately. This includes situations involving safety risks, security concerns, major property damage, or other actions that may harm the lounge, its users, or the campus community.

Before access is removed, when appropriate, \[designated lounge administrator\] will review the concern and any available information. The individual student or registered student organization may be given a chance to respond before a final decision is made.

If access is removed, the individual student or registered student organization will be notified by email. The notice should explain the reason for removal and the effective date.

For individual students, removal of access is permanent.

For registered student organizations, removal of access will remain in place for at least one year. After one year, the organization may submit a written request to \[designated lounge administrator\] asking for access to be restored.

Restoration of access is not guaranteed. The request may be reviewed based on the reason access was removed, any corrective action taken, and the organization’s ability to follow the Terms of Use in the future.

## **Terms for Conference Room Reservation**

Users of the conference room can book up to 2 hours a day on (insert campus reservation system) using 30 minute segments. There is no limit to the number of days in a week a student may book the room. If there is no one who has booked the conference room, the room assumes a first-come first-serve basis, until someone reserves the room.

A QR code will be kept on the outside door of the conference room linked to the reservation system.

**Leaving items in the room unattended is not a valid method of reserving the room based on a first come first serve basis.**
