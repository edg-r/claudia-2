## What Reads AI or Generic

The prose is often too evenly polished. Several paragraphs move through claim, explanation, and conclusion with little friction. Edgar's Blue Memo has more asymmetric rhythm: long circumstance sentence, blunt correction, fragment, then a final analytical punch. The Orange Memo rarely risks that shape.

The introduction has a few mechanical tells that will read as rushed or machine-assisted: "Their claim is not that are more prone to civil war," "Cederman et als findings," and "quantitatively prove this danger." The first is a missing subject. The second needs an apostrophe. The third overstates what a quantitative article can do. "Show" or "find" is more Edgar and more academically careful.

Some diction is abstract and generic: "expanded competition over governmental power," "created space for electoral politics," "institutional position," "the state apparatus of coercion," "the deeper mechanism." These phrases are not wrong, but they lack Edgar's concrete verbs. The Blue Memo says Bush "shifted," Hussein "could not surrender," ambiguity "collapsed" deterrence, and commitments "pushed" actors into war. Orange needs more of that physical pressure.

The paragraph about actor preferences is useful but list-like. "First preference / fallback / worst outcome" is analytically neat, but it sounds like a model answer. Edgar would likely compress the inventory and spend more voice on the collision between the two preference orders.

2. **Replace abstract nouns with physical verbs.** Swap phrases like "expanded competition over governmental power" for "opened a fight over who could command the state." Swap "remained outside civilian control" for "kept the guns outside civilian hands." The idea is not to get casual. The idea is to make the state feel acted on.

3. **Restore a few short terminal fragments or near-fragments.** The memo already has "It escalated." Add one or two Blue Memo-style landings where the argument warrants it, such as "A veto with guns behind it." Use sparingly. This is the fastest way to move from clean agent prose toward Edgar's rhythm.

4. **Compress the preference inventory into collision language.** The current paragraph lists both actors cleanly. A more Edgar move would say the preferences ran directly into each other: Suu Kyi needed electoral legitimacy to become authority; Min Aung Hlaing needed electoral legitimacy to stay harmless. The 2020 landslide made both impossible at once.
