Gauss-Markov Assumption Checklist

assumptionhow_checkedtakeaway
1. Linear in parametersResidual-vs-fitted plot and quadratic term checkIf the squared term is insignificant and the residual plot is roughly patternless, linearity is more plausible
2. No multicollinearityVariance inflation factors (VIF)Low VIF values suggest multicollinearity is not severe
3. Random samplingQualitative caveat: cross-country observational sample, not guaranteed randomCannot be fully verified with the dataset; discuss as a limitation
4. Exogeneity / Zero conditional meanQualitative caveat: omitted variables and reverse causality may remainCannot be proven with the data; discuss controls plus remaining endogeneity risk
5. HeteroskedasticityBreusch-Pagan-type and White-type tests; HC1 robust SE appliedIf variance is not constant, robust SEs address inference
6. Bonus: Normal distributionShapiro-Wilk test on residualsUseful descriptive check, but not required for Gauss-Markov
