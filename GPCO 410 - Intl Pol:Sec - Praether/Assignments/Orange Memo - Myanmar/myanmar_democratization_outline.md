The **Tatmadaw** preferred regime survival, institutional autonomy, constitutional veto power, and protection from civilian oversight. The NLD’s preference was to convert democratic legitimacy into governing authority. The Tatmadaw’s preference was to preserve its veto position and prevent civilian control over security institutions. As the NLD repeatedly won elections, the military’s expected payoff from continued democratization declined. The transition became less acceptable to the actor that still controlled force.
